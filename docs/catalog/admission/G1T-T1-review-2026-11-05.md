## T1-01 — Customer Complaint Management

**Finding: PASS**

**Five questions:** Yes / Yes / Yes / Yes / Yes.

**Reviewer notes:**

The organization is the appropriate bearer. Its relevant members, organized to receive, route, resolve and respond to complaints, constitute the proposed material basis. The four indexed acts represent organizational exercise.

BPO's `CustomerComplaintManagementCapability` includes complaint analysis (`P19072`), while this BCO candidate excludes it. These should be treated as distinct capability types with different functional scopes, not as equivalent classes or interchangeable designations. The BCO candidate is restricted to operational complaint handling. No subclass relationship to the BPO class is established by that observation.

`P10399` is admissible under question 4. Its BPO definition concerns correcting a legitimate complaint and documenting the resolution. The operational correction and its descriptive record are distinguishable from any separate act granting a legal entitlement or exercising authorization. Retain `P10399`; do not treat it as evidence of permission-granting capability.

The exclusion of `P19072` is justified because the source describes analysis for service improvement and customer profiling, not the handling of the particular complaint.

**Worked cases:** Accepted. The records distinguish loss of permission from loss of ability, loss of coordination from loss of an enabling system, and provider performance from internal organizational performance.

**Required record clarification:** Correct the BPO terminology relationship to avoid implying identity or equivalence.

**Index:** Retain all four acts. No re-indexing.

## T1-02 — Payroll Processing

**Finding: PASS**

**Five questions:** Yes / Yes / Yes / Yes / Yes.

**Reviewer notes:**

The capability is organizational, grounded in members carrying out pay determination, deductions, disbursement, payroll accounting and reporting through organizational arrangements.

Retain `P10865`, responding to employee payroll inquiries. Answering questions about salary calculations, deductions, payments or adjustments is an operational activity within the deliberately defined scope of this capability. This does not mean that a generic inquiry-response skill establishes payroll-processing capacity.

Question 2 passes at the type level. Historically feasible manual payroll processing demonstrates that a particular electronic payroll system is not necessarily constitutive. This does not establish that manual operation is feasible for every organization at every scale. Where irreversible system loss leaves no feasible alternative, CD2 requires escalation back to the basis-kind question rather than automatic continued attribution.

The outsourced-payroll worked case correctly applies CAP-34. An organization's member who only provides inputs to a bureau and receives its outputs participates in the provider's process but does not thereby perform the payroll acts. Sourcing is not proof of internal capability.

Policy formulation, tax planning, time reporting and summary acts remain appropriately excluded. Payroll tax calculation, payment and reporting remain included. Filing a tax report may discharge an obligation without constituting an authorization act.

**Worked cases:** Accepted, with the scale-dependent CD2 escalation explicitly preserved.

**Required record clarification:** State that manual feasibility is not a universal claim about every organizational instance.

**Index:** Retain all eleven acts. No re-indexing.

## T1-03 — Accounts Payable Processing

**Finding: PASS**

**Five questions:** Yes / Yes / Yes / Yes / Yes.

**Reviewer notes:**

The organization is the appropriate bearer. Members who verify payable obligations, resolve exceptions, process payments and maintain payable accounts can constitute its material basis.

Retain `P10877`, responding to AP inquiries, using the same criterion applied to payroll `P10865`. Answering questions about amounts, payment dates, discounts, installments or exceptions is part of the defined operational scope when it exercises the organization's payables capability. Generic communication skill alone does not establish this capability.

Question 3 should not depend on the claim that work necessarily crosses purchasing, receiving and treasury positions. Cross-position coordination is common but not a universal condition. The essential criterion is that the acts are performed through the organization's arrangements on its behalf, including arrangements consisting of a single member.

With `P10872` excluded, the withdrawn-authority case is coherent. Verification, exception resolution and accounting capability can persist after authority to release a payment is withdrawn. Execution of an otherwise authorized payment is operational; granting that authorization is distinct.

For inclusiveness, amend the definition so that purchase-order matching is required only where a purchase order applies. Legitimate non-PO supplier invoices should not be excluded by the wording. The retained invoice-auditing and exception-resolution acts support this clarification without introducing new evidence.

The other five excluded acts remain correctly outside the realization index.

**Worked cases:** Accepted, with the distinction between payment authorization and operational ability preserved.

**Required record clarifications:** Correct the cross-position claim in question 3 and qualify purchase-order matching in the definition.

**Index:** Retain all seven acts. No re-indexing.
