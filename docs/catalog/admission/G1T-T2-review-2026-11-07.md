## 4. The eight open questions

**OQ-1. T2-02 Corporate Card Management, question 4. Not met as drafted; met once corrected → PENDING REVIEW.**
The three exclusions are right. Setting card policies and approval limits (`P20930`),
approving or changing limits (`P20934`) and cancelling cards (`P20935`) each grant, alter
or withdraw a holder's standing to spend. Two defects remain:
- The definition still says the capability will "set their terms". A card's terms are its
  limits and use conditions, which are exactly the standing the index excludes. Under CD7
  the definition must not carry what the index removed.
- The designating summary act `P20929` (PCF 9.6.3, six children) has those three Q4 acts as
  parts. Its PCF description is "handling and authoring [authorizing] credit cards", so it
  is not describable without standing.

What remains is non-deontic: processing card requests (`P20931`), read as handling the
application and not deciding it; ordering cards from the issuer (`P20932`), with an
acceptable CAP-06 reason; and managing card accounts (`P20933`). Corrections: remove
`P20929` (Q4: its parts grant and withdraw standing); delete "set their terms" from the
definition and the grounding. With those applied, I would find PASS.

**OQ-2. T2-04 Capital Planning, the one-act index. Accept a thin index; do not hold for BPO; narrow the definition → PENDING REVIEW.**
CD4 requires one or more index members, and six other classes in this batch have one, so
thinness alone is no ground to hold. Waiting for BPO to separate planning from approval
would put an upstream change in the critical path for no gain. The index act `P10847`
(financial justification for project approval) is non-deontic: it prepares the case on
which approval is made and is not the approval. The four exclusions are right (three join
approval, and one is policy, Q5). The defect is the definition. "Identify ... proposed
capital investments" and "budget for them" have no member, because the only budgeting act
(`P10845`) joins budgeting with approval and is excluded whole. The definition must be
narrowed, for example: "A business capability of an organization to evaluate the
financial case for proposed capital investments, on which a decision to proceed is made,
grounded in the organization's members as organized." That makes the class capital
investment appraisal. Whether to keep the label "Capital Planning" is the architect's
decision, so the finding is PENDING. Once the narrowed definition is accepted, it is a PASS.

**OQ-3. T2-06 Financial Accounting, capability area or type. Treat it as a capability area; withdraw it from the batch → PENDING REVIEW.**
Three independent reasons:
- Its only member, `P10730` (PCF 9.3, level 2), is the summary act over four components. The
  signed tranche-1 practice excludes summary acts, including a class's own: `P10732`
  (9.5) for T1-02 and `P10756` (9.6.1) for T1-03 (`docs/catalog/tranche-1.md` §4). The
  first part of 9.3 includes "Set and enforce approval limits" (`P10817`, Q4).
- Without the summary act the index is empty, and CD4 cannot be met.
- `tranche-2.md` already withholds the structurally identical level-2 groupings 9.4 Capital
  Expenditure Management and 9.6 Accounts Payable and Expense Reimbursement Management as
  capability areas (CD16, E4). Admitting 9.3 as a type would be inconsistent with that.

Its components (T2-07, T2-08, T2-09, T2-10) stand on their own indexes, and their part-of
links to an area are harmless.

**OQ-4. T2-07 Accounting Policy Management, question 4. Met, on the Formulation reading, with a wording correction → PASS.**
- **The reading.** BPO types `P10816` (establish accounting policies) as an Act of
  Formulation. Its output is a directive information content entity. Bringing a policy into
  force with authority is a separate act, and it is not indexed.
- **Consistent with tranche 1.** T1-02's signed record treats policy-setting (`P10853`) as
  formulation under question 5, not as deontic under question 4.
- **Publishing.** `P20604` (publish accounting policies) is communication.
- **The correction.** The definition's verb "set" is ambiguous between formulating and
  adopting with authority. Replace "set, publish and maintain" with "formulate, publish and
  revise". Record in the record that adoption with authority is outside the index.

If the named reviewer reads "establish" as adoption with authority, question 4 fails.
There is then no non-deontic act to re-index to, since BPO's class cannot be split, and the
class would have to be reclassified.

**OQ-5. T2-17 Internal Control Design, question 4. Met for the three leaf acts; not met for the summary act; the class needs a scope decision → PENDING REVIEW.**
Defining a code of ethics (`P10915`), process objectives and risks (`P11250`) and risk
tolerances (`P11251`) are formulations, describable without standing. A risk tolerance is
a quantitative appetite statement. It is not an approval limit (compare `P10817`, rightly
excluded as Q4 in T2-07), because it says what the organization is prepared to bear, not
who may approve what.

The designating act `P10762` (PCF 9.8.1) is a different matter. Its parts include
`P10916` (assign roles and responsibility, which the draft itself excludes as Q4) and
`P10914` (establish a board and audit committee, an Act of Organizational Establishment,
CAP-06). It is neither non-deontic nor purely exercise, and must leave the index. That
leaves no member that defines a control, so the definition's head clause ("define the
controls, policies and procedures") is unsupported. The architect should choose one of:
- narrow the definition to the leaf acts, as a "control environment and risk-objective
  definition" capability; or
- hold the class until BPO has an act type for designing controls.

**OQ-6. T2-27 Revenue and Receivables Management, capability area or type. Treat it as a capability area; withdraw it from the batch → PENDING REVIEW.**
This is a stronger case than T2-06:
- The definition is a five-part conjunction (recognize revenue, extend credit, invoice,
  record, collect). Its five parts are the five component classes T2-28 to T2-32 and T2-31.
- Its only member, `P10729` (PCF 9.2, level 2), is a summary act. Its PCF description
  (comparing revenue targets with reality, and reviewing a year's entries) matches none of
  the definition's parts.
- The index is empty once the summary act is excluded, as tranche-1 practice requires.
- It is parallel to the withheld areas 9.4 and 9.6.

**OQ-7. T2-31 Customer Credit Management, question 4. Not met as drafted → PENDING REVIEW.**
The draft reads "set and review the credit it extends to each" as an amount the organization
decides, describable without standing. That is inconsistent with T2-02, where the draft
itself excludes "Approve/Change credit limits" (`P20934`) as conferring or altering a
holder's standing to spend. A customer's credit limit is the customer's standing to buy on
credit up to that amount, exactly as a card limit is a holder's standing to spend. The
analysis that recommends an amount is non-deontic; setting it is a grant.

Corrections:
- Reword the definition to "evaluate requests from customers to buy on credit and the
  credit to be extended to each, review existing accounts, and monitor how credit is
  used".
- Remove the summary act `P10742` (PCF 9.2.1). Its parts include `P10790` and `P10793`,
  both Q4.
- Move `P14188` (forecast credit scoring requirement) out under Q5. Its PCF description
  ("planning credit score requirements based on established credit policies") sets the
  criteria that govern this capability's own decisions, as `P10789` does.

The remaining three acts (`P14187`, `P10791`, `P10792`) are non-deontic and support the
corrected definition.

**OQ-8. T2-42 Treasury Policy Management, question 4. Met, on the same Formulation reading as OQ-4 → PASS.**
- **The policy acts.** `P10886` (establish and publish treasury policies), `P10887` (develop
  procedures) and `P10890` (revise procedures) are Acts of Formulation. `P10888` (monitor
  procedures) and `P10889` (audit procedures) are a monitoring and an appraisal act. None
  grants standing.
- **The exclusion.** `P10885` is correctly out. Its PCF description ("selecting ... the
  authoritative body for investments") is also Q4, not only Q5, and the record should say
  so.
- **The correction.** Replace "set" with "formulate" in the definition, and record the
  reading as for T2-07.
- **A note on `P10889`.** "Auditing the treasury function" borders on internal-audit
  assurance. I accept it because PCF places it under 9.7.1 as the step that feeds revision
  (`P10890`), which supports "keep current". A reviewer who reads it as internal audit
  should remove it as OTHER. That would not change the finding.

**MD-3. The question 2 and question 3 answers are boilerplate.**
Every Q2 answer ends "the work can be done with other tools, so none is constitutive of the
type". Every Q3 answer reads "through its division of work and its accounts and records".
- **Q2.** The tranche-1 review required that the claim be stated as one about the type, not
  about every organization, and that the CD2 escalation be preserved where an irreversible
  loss leaves no feasible alternative. Each record should name the alternative (for
  example, manual screening against published lists for T2-13, or telephone dealing for
  T2-39 to T2-41) and add the non-universality caveat.
- **Q3.** "Division of work" implies several positions, which the tranche-1 review rejected
  for T1-03. Reword it to: "realizations are acts performed on the organization's behalf
  through its arrangements, which may consist of a single member (b1)".

**MD-4. The batch rule refuses kind-of children's acts to the broader index (section 1: "whether the link is `part-of` or `kind-of`").**
The rule is right for part-of. It is not right for kind-of. A subtype's instances are
instances of the supertype, so their realizations are realizations of a supertype
instance. If the supertype's index omits them, CAP-21 rejects realizations of a capability
asserted at the supertype that its own subtype admits. CD4's non-exhaustiveness means this
is not a contradiction, but it is an incoherent index. In this batch the rule bites only on
T2-37 (three kind-of children inside the batch). Correction: revise the rule so a kind-of
supertype's index includes its subtypes' members, or reclassify the links. For T2-37, I
recommend withdrawing it as an area (section 6 note on T2-37, and section 7).

**MD-5. Exclusion codes are inconsistent for the same kind of act.**
"Establish ... policies and approval limits" is coded Q4 in T2-02 (`P20930`) and Q5 in
T2-03 (`P10880`). Both should carry Q5 with Q4 also noted, as T2-03's reason already says.
`P10885` (T2-42) should note Q4 as well as Q5.

**MD-6. Acts excluded as "another concept's work" are left orphaned.**
Each is excluded as belonging to a named concept whose index does not hold it:
- `P10852` and `P11175` (named for Financial Performance Management);
- `P10930` and `P14075` (Tax Planning);
- `P10929` (Tax Processing);
- `P10891` and `P10919` (Internal Control Design);
- `P11215` (Counterparty Credit Exposure Management).

This is not a G1-T defect, since each index is drawn from the class's own evidence. But the
reasons point to an assignment that no record has made. Recommend that the tranche-2
review assign them, as it already did for six acts, at the next revision.
