## The question put

> How should the parent's definition be aligned to the ratified spec?

The two answers offered were "Make v0.5 in force" and "Revise the parent only". The
first was described as: "This regenerates all nine metamodel definitions from the
ratified spec, each as a recorded revision. It also changes which spec names the
vendoring tool reads."

## The decision

> Make v0.5 in force

## What it does

- `specifications/Business-Capability-Ontology-v0.5.0-draft.3.md` takes the layout role
  `specification`, and `v0.4.0-draft.1` becomes `documentation`, kept as the prior
  version.
- Each of the nine metamodel records names its v0.5 row, and `revises` its previous
  record digest with a change note. Their definitions are regenerated from v0.5's §4.
  Four definitions change text: Business Capability (which now reads as the D5 record's
  alignment table requires), Capability Assessment, Capability Maturity Rating and
  Capability Sourcing Assessment. Business Capability's and Capability Assessment's
  "Does not imply" cells change too. The other five rows read as they did, apart from
  the `bco:` prefix.
- `tools/vocab/spec_terms.py` reads v0.5's §4: it accepts `bco:` beside v0.4's `ex:`,
  the family row written in plain words, the Functional Adequacy Assessment row (no IRI;
  "representation open"), excluded with its reason, and the prose after the parent
  row's Parents. It still reads v0.4 exactly as before, because the build re-reads older
  commits to describe prior records. Every v0.4 row's digest is unchanged.
- The vendoring tool's seeds come from v0.5. The extracts are regenerated by their
  generator.

## What it does not do

- It ratifies no vocabulary concept. The nine stay Draft.
- It writes no OWL and mints no ontology term. D6 still needs its own authorization.
- It does not reopen D5 or any G1-T record. The 39 specific concepts' definitions are
  their own and do not change.
- It changes no BPO class and does not re-pin BPO.
