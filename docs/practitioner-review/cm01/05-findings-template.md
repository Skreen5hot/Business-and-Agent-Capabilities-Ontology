**Concerns:** name the concept, pattern (P1 to P5), section, scenario (S1 to S5), decision (DP-1, DP-2) or specification item the finding is about.

**Supporting example:** a real or realistic case from your experience, anonymized as needed, that shows the problem or the point.

**Severity**, from your perspective as a practitioner:

| Severity | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Critical | The model would lead practitioners to a wrong planning conclusion in common cases, or cannot represent something essential to capability planning |
| High | A significant distinction or case is wrong, or unclear enough that practitioners would likely misapply it |
| Medium | A gap or ambiguity with a workable workaround, or one that affects less common cases |
| Low | Wording, terminology or presentation |
| Observation | A comment or question, with no change requested |

**Recommendation:** what you would change, add or remove. A direction is enough; you are not asked to write model text.
