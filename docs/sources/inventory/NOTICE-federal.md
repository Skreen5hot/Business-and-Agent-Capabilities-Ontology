## Federal Integrated Business Framework (FIBF)

Federal Integrated Business Framework business standards, published by the U.S. General Services Administration (Office of Shared Solutions and Performance Improvement) at https://ussm.gsa.gov/fibf/ for each domain's standards lead agency. Works of the United States Government; no endorsement by GSA or any agency is implied.

- licence: `LicenseRef-US-Government-Work` (works of the United States
  Government; GSA's website policies are pinned in `rights.yaml`)
- decisions: FIBF-1 c, FIBF-2 a, FIBF-3 a (`docs/decisions/2026-10-28-i2a-decided.md`)
- redistributed in: `docs/sources/inventory/fibf-entries.yaml`, `docs/sources/inventory/fibf-business-capabilities.yaml`
- the documents, their status and digests: `docs/sources/inventory/fibf-snapshot.yaml`

Workbooks that carry a copyright line of The MITRE Corporation are
not harvested (FIBF-1 c), and no text equal to theirs is published
from another file. Columns that map entries to ITIL (AXELOS,
PeopleCert) or to the TBM Taxonomy (the TBM Council) are not read
(FIBF-2 a). Those names are the rightsholders' own; nothing of
theirs is redistributed here.
