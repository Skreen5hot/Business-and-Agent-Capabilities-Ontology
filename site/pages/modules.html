Upstream extracts

Partial extracts of the ontologies the BCO builds on: the terms this repository or its specification names, plus their named ancestry. They are not the upstream ontologies. Every axiom whose object is an anonymous class expression is dropped, so these files cannot support a claim of consistency with BFO, CCO or BPO.
